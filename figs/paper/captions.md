## fig1

**The problem and the method.** (a) Normalised TICs of three samples from each of the 5 fish oil batches (apr2016 n=10, aug2015 n=2, jan2016 n=26, jul2016 n=16, sep2015 n=36), each on its own retention-time axis. Shaded: before 5 min, when not every run had started acquiring; excluded from every score. Triangles joined by dashed lines: the same three compounds in every batch (shown 10–32 min). (b) The same TICs mapped into the shared frame by the joint warps (gauge: sep2015). (c) The shared frame drawn from the fitted warps: five compounds (black, shared clock) and where each batch sees them. Every batch has one monotone warp f_k into the shared clock, so the warp between any two batches is f_l⁻¹ ∘ f_k and all pairwise warps are mutually consistent.

## fig2

**Anchors, fits and recovery of known drift.** (a) The 4,396 anchors between sep2015 and jul2016 (grey; red rings: weight 0 after robust reweighting, 2.5%) with the joint warp and its 90 % bootstrap band, the same fitter on this pair's anchors alone (pairwise smooth) and PCHIP through the median anchors (dense PCHIP). (b) Synthetic batches (src/synthetic.py) with known drift: true shift into sep2015 (thick, faint) and the joint estimate (thin), fitted exactly as on the real data (3,811 anchors, λ = 100 by RT-blocked cross-validation). (c) Absolute error of the joint warp for every batch pair where compounds exist in both batches: median (dot) and 5–95th percentile. Median 0.023 min, 95th percentile 0.075 min, maximum 0.107 min (one RT bin is 0.225 min).

## fig3

**Alignment quality.** (a) TIC r of every batch-A sample against batch B (mean over B samples) after warping A into B's frame, on the common acquisition window; bars are pair means, dotted lines the unaligned mean. Means over drifted pairs: joint 0.598, pairwise smooth 0.572, dense PCHIP 0.558, pooled-anchor PCHIP 0.531, unaligned 0.066; near-aligned pairs: joint 0.779, unaligned 0.755. (b) Cross-fitted held-out anchor error (folds over samples), median per batch pair; over all pairs dense PCHIP 0.035, pairwise smooth 0.052, joint 0.060 min.

## fig4

**Transitivity.** (a) Disagreement between composing two pairwise warps and the direct warp, |g_bc(g_ab(t)) − g_ac(t)| averaged over 5–40 min, for all 60 ordered batch triplets. Pairwise warps: mean 0.116 min, maximum 0.277 min; joint warps: maximum 0.0023 min (numerical). (b) The worst route, jul2016 → aug2015 → jan2016: the direct pairwise warp (solid) and the composed one (dashed) differ (shaded); the joint warp is the same along every route by construction.

## fig5

**Borrowing strength across batches.** (a) TIC r of each evaluated pair with the joint model fitted on all anchors (filled) and refitted without any anchor of that pair (open); crosses: fitted only on anchors against the reference batch sep2015 (star design, pairs not involving it). Removing a pair's anchors changes its TIC r by at most 0.007 on 9 of 10 pairs and by 0.058 on sep2015 × jan2016. (b) That pair: its own anchors (grey), the joint warp with them and without them; median error against its own anchors rises from 0.061 to 0.088 min.

## fig6

**Fair benchmark.** Every method reduced to the correspondence map it estimates (RT in B → RT in A) and scored the same way: (a) A warped into B's frame; (b) both batches resampled into the frame halfway between them. Mean TIC r per group of pairs. Drifted pairs: best is Joint (0.601) in B's frame and Neural joint (0.673) at the midpoint. The WarpTransformer is gcmsalign's v2 model with its own anchor calibration (seed 0). COW-TIC fits a separate warp per sample pair to maximise TIC r. Blue: this work.

## figS1

**Choosing λ.** (a) Median held-out anchor error under RT-blocked cross-validation (whole 12-bin RT windows held out) for both gauges; dotted: selected λ = 10 (reference gauge, 0.111 min; the mean gauge reaches 0.096 min there). (b) Mean TIC r against λ, computed after the fact; for λ ≥ 1 the drifted-pair mean stays within 0.595–0.600.

## figS2

**Uncertainty.** (a) Share of held-out anchors inside their 90 % interval (cross-fitted): the bootstrap band of the warp alone covers 15%, the predictive interval (band combined with the pair's robust anchor scatter) 81%, against the 90 % target. (b) Width of the 90 % bootstrap band (200 refits, samples resampled within batches) against the number of direct anchors nearby, every batch pair and RT bin between 2 and 43 min where the warp is not clamped at a run end (the same rule as script 30); Spearman ρ = -0.40.
